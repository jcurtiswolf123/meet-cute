The flow in one minute

Mutuals is operator-led. Members do not browse or swipe through the roster. You choose a thoughtful pair, each person decides privately, and the platform connects them only after mutual consent.

  1. A prospective member applies, or you add someone who already asked to be matched.
  2. You approve the applicant and confirm that each person is ready to match.
  3. You choose two people in Matchmaking and send one private introduction to each.
  4. Each person views the other profile and privately chooses Yes or No.
  5. If both say yes, Mutuals connects them by email. If either passes, the introduction closes quietly.
  6. You monitor the pair and coordinate any date details manually.
Use email as the production channel. As of July 24, 2026, carrier approval for Mutuals SMS is not complete. Do not depend on text invites, group texts, or text feedback requests until the SMS campaign is approved and delivery is verified.

Know the Studio

Matchmaking

Choose two ready people, write what each person sees, send the introductions, and manage the result.

Conversations

See private decision state, health, recent activity, and the introduction transcript.

Directory

Review applicants, search members, open profiles, read notes and history, and handle delivery failures.

Status

Track suggestions, mutual interest, dates, and relationships. A suggestion is not a sent introduction.

Events

Create a dinner or gathering, select members, email invitations, and manage RSVP state.

Co-pilot

Ask roster questions or run supported actions. Commands under Do change live data.

Team

Add or revoke operators. Each person signs in with their own email and one-time link.

Status and Matchmaking do different jobs. The manual match form in Status creates an internal suggestion. It does not contact either person. Send the real introduction from Matchmaking.

Invite someone to apply

For normal member onboarding, send:

https://hellomutuals.com/apply

I would love to invite you to Mutuals. It is a curated matchmaking community in NYC and San Francisco. Start here: https://hellomutuals.com/apply. You will receive a one-time email link, then the application takes a few minutes.

What happens next

  1. The person enters their email and opens the one-time link.
  2. They complete the application, legal consent, and optional SMS consent.
  3. The completed application appears in Directory under New applicants.
  4. You review the profile and choose Approve or Decline.
  5. After approval, the member signs in at /login and clicks Opt in to get matched on Home.

Approval does not automatically mark a member ready. The member must opt in from the member app.

Quick-add someone who already consented

  1. Open Matchmaking and expand Add someone to match.
  2. Enter their name, city, and email. Add phone and social links when available.
  3. Add a short note that will help with matching.
  4. Check matchmaking consent only if the person personally asked to be added.
  5. Check SMS consent only if the person separately agreed to Mutuals texts.
  6. Click Add person.

Quick-add marks the person ready. It does not send a welcome email. To give them member-app access, send https://hellomutuals.com/login and have them use the same email you entered.

Do not infer consent. A referral, a friend's request, or a phone number in your contacts is not enough to check either consent box.

Prepare a member before matching

Open the member from Directory and check:

  • The account is active and the person is ready to match.
  • City, age, and email are accurate.
  • The approved photo is current.
  • The profile gives the proposed match enough context.
  • The recommendation and voucher information are useful.
  • There are no blocks, safety concerns, or conflicting notes.
  • Recent match history does not create an overlap or repeat.

If the person is in Directory but not in the Matchmaking picker, ask them to sign in at /login, open Home, and opt in.

Choose and send a match

  1. Open Matchmaking.
  2. Choose the first person, then the second person.
  3. Review the prefilled notes.
  4. Write About the first person. The second person sees this.
  5. Write About the second person. The first person sees this.
  6. Add an optional one-line rationale when it helps explain the fit.
  7. Read both previews and verify names, profile details, and delivery channels.
  8. Click Send introductions.
The send is live. There is no second confirmation screen after you click Send introductions.

What to write

Use two or three concrete details: what the person does or cares about, a specific interest, and why the pair might connect. Do not include internal notes, medical information, or anything the person did not agree to share.

What each person receives

Mutuals sends two separate private emails. Each email names the proposed match, links to a private profile page, and offers Yes, introduce us or No thanks. The recipient can also reply Y or N.

  • The first yes stays private while Mutuals waits for the other person.
  • If both say yes, Mutuals connects them by email.
  • If either passes, the introduction closes.
  • The other person is not told who passed.
  • The private profile link is unique and should not be forwarded.

After mutual consent, the pair chooses how and when to meet. Mutuals does not automatically reserve a venue or schedule the date.

Use the operator controls correctly

Resend

Resend contacts only the person still pending. It replaces the older private email link, so the person should use the newest email. Conversations offers bulk resend after at least three days without a reply.

Close

Close an introduction that is no longer appropriate or has gone quiet. Closing cancels outstanding delivery work. Conversations offers bulk close after at least 14 days of silence.

Connect now

Connect now forces both decisions to yes and sends the connection. Use it only when both people already gave explicit approval outside the platform. Never use it to skip private consent.

Ask for feedback and Jump in

These controls currently rely on authorized SMS. Until SMS delivery is approved and verified, follow up by email or direct conversation. Record useful feedback in the member profile notes.

Handle failed deliveries

  1. Open Directory and read the delivery failure.
  2. Check the email spelling and account status.
  3. Confirm the match remains active and authorized.
  4. Fix the underlying issue.
  5. Use Retry once.

Do not repeatedly retry an SMS failure while carrier approval is pending. Use email. The system will cancel delivery if the account, block, match, consent, or private link state is no longer valid.

Invite another operator

  1. Open Team.
  2. Enter the person's full name, exact email, and city.
  3. Click Add & invite.
  4. Confirm the success message.

If the account is added but the invitation email fails, send the new operator to https://hellomutuals.com/studio/login. They should use the exact email entered in Team.

If the email already belongs to a member, adding the person as an operator promotes the existing account and clears old sessions. They must sign in again.

Revoke access signs out an ordinary operator and removes Studio access. It does not delete the person's record. Jess cannot revoke herself or another protected super admin.

Invite members to an event

  1. Open Events and create the event.
  2. Open the event and select eligible active members.
  3. Click Add & email selected.
  4. Track each person as Invited, Confirmed, Attended, or No-show.

The invitation email is automatic. Venue booking, payment, seating, dietary follow-up, and calendar coordination are manual.

A practical daily routine

  1. Review new applicants in Directory.
  2. Check failed deliveries before sending new work.
  3. Open Conversations and handle anything marked Needs attention.
  4. Review members who are ready to match.
  5. Create introductions from Matchmaking.
  6. Follow up on mutual connections manually.
  7. Record notes that will improve future matching.
  8. Review upcoming event guest lists.

Operator safety rules

  • Never share an operator magic link or use a shared account.
  • Never add someone to matchmaking without their direct permission.
  • Never check SMS consent unless the person separately agreed to texts.
  • Never use Connect now without explicit yes from both people.
  • Never tell one person who passed.
  • Never share private Studio notes with a member or proposed match.
  • Never depend on SMS while carrier approval is pending.
  • Never claim a venue or date is booked unless a human confirmed it.
  • Use Co-pilot carefully. Commands can change live data.

Quick troubleshooting

I cannot sign in

Use /studio/login, request a fresh link, and open it within 15 minutes. A link works once.

A member is in Directory but not in Matchmaking

They are probably not ready. Ask them to sign in at /login, open Home, and opt in.

A person never received an introduction

Check Directory for a failed delivery, confirm the email, and use Resend after fixing the issue. Do not assume an SMS was delivered.

The pair appears in Status but received nothing

Status can contain internal suggestions. Create and send the real introduction from Matchmaking.

Only one person said yes

Wait. Their answer stays private. After three days, use Resend for the pending person if appropriate.

Both said yes but they are not connected

Check Directory for a connection delivery failure. Fix the email issue and retry. Do not create a duplicate match.

The normal operating rule: email first, private consent for both people, manual date coordination, and notes recorded after every useful conversation.
