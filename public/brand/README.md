# Logo marks

Three monochrome candidates generated 2026-07-29 (Gemini 2.5 Flash Image),
1024x1024 PNG on white. Nothing here is wired into the site yet.

- `logo-arcs.png` (recommended). Two half-arcs with a deliberate gap. Reads as an
  introduction that has not been made yet, which is what the product actually
  does: it puts two people one decision apart. Survives being shrunk to a
  favicon, and works as a single flat colour.
- `logo-overlap.png`. Two circles overlapping at one point, vesica piscis.
- `logo-figures.png`. Two abstract figures. Legible but closer to generic
  corporate "teamwork" clipart than the other two.

Before shipping one: redraw the chosen mark as an SVG. These are raster
generations, so they have soft edges and no true vector geometry, and the
wordmark should be set in real type rather than generated.
